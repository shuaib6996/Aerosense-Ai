Complete Hand Gesture Guide

Reliable finger-fold & pinch detection

☝️

1 Finger Fold & Open → Next

Fold Index finger down and open it to scroll to Next Reel / Short.

✌️

2 Fingers Fold & Open → Previous

Fold Index + Middle fingers together and open them for Previous Reel.

⬅️

3 Fingers Fold & Open → System Back

Fold Index, Middle & Ring fingers and open to navigate Back.

🏠

4 Fingers Fold & Open → Home Screen

Close all 4 fingers into a gentle fist and open palm to go Home.

⏱️

1 Finger Fold & Hold (0.65s) → Action Menu

Hold 1 finger folded to open the floating menu (Volume, Play/Pause, Back, Home).

👌

Pinch (Thumb + Index) → Air Tap

Touch Thumb & Index tips together to click any button under the Air Cursor.
