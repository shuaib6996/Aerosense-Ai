## 1. Introduction

AeroSense AI Labs ("we", "us", or "our") respects the privacy of our users ("user", "you"). This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application and Progressive Web App (**AeroSense AI™**) collect, use, and protect your information when using touchless camera gesture recognition, voice commands, and media services.

---

## 2. 100% On-Device Camera Processing (Google Play Policy Compliance)

- **Purpose of Camera Access**: AeroSense AI requests camera permission (`android.permission.CAMERA`) strictly for detecting 21 real-time hand landmark coordinates to interpret air gestures (e.g., pinch, swipe, peace sign, fist).
- **No Video Recording or Image Transmission**: Camera video frames are processed ephemeral and client-side on your local device CPU/GPU utilizing Google MediaPipe. 
- **Zero Cloud Storage**: **No video frames, camera pictures, or biometric facial/hand data are ever recorded, saved to disk, or transmitted to any external server, cloud service, or third party.**
- **Camera Standby Mode**: When Vision mode is toggled off, the camera stream immediately terminates and hardware sensors are freed.

---

## 3. Microphone & Voice Commands Disclosure

- **Purpose of Microphone Access**: Voice navigation keywords (`"Next"`, `"Pause"`, `"Play"`, `"Like"`, `"Volume Up"`) are analyzed only when the user explicitly taps the "Voice" assistant button.
- **Speech Processing**: Audio is processed ephemerally using standard local Web Speech APIs solely to match navigation intent. Audio recordings are never stored, saved, or uploaded to any third party.

---

## 4. Local Storage & Cookies

AeroSense AI uses local browser storage (`localStorage` / `CacheStorage`) strictly for:
- Caching offline application assets (`index.html`, `admin.html`, `manifest.json`, `sw.js`).
- Storing user preferences (e.g., custom gesture mapping, pinch sensitivity, volume level).

No third-party trackers, invasive advertising SDKs, or unauthorized profiling cookies are embedded in the application.

---

## 5. Security & Safety

- **Safety Warning**: Users are cautioned not to use touchless gestures or distracted interaction while driving a motor vehicle, operating heavy machinery, or engaging in activities where full physical attention is required.
- **Children's Privacy**: AeroSense AI does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
