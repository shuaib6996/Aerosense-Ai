Privacy Policy

AeroSense AI Version 1

1. Summary

AeroSense AI is designed with strict on-device privacy. Your physical movements, camera video, and voice commands remain 100% private to your phone.

2. Camera & Vision Data

AeroSense AI accesses your front camera solely to compute real-time hand skeleton landmarks. No video frames, photographs, or recordings are ever saved, stored, or sent to any cloud server. All processing occurs strictly in volatile device memory (RAM) and is discarded immediately.

3. Accessibility Service

The Android Accessibility Service is used strictly to simulate physical touches and system navigation (Back, Home, Reel Swiping) based on your air gestures. We do not read, log, or inspect screen text, passwords, or personal data.

4. Voice Recognition

Voice control triggers (e.g. "Next", "Back", "Home") use Android's native speech recognition. Audio transcripts are parsed in real time and are never stored or shared.

5. Zero Personal Data Collection

AeroSense AI does not collect names, emails, phone numbers, location, device IDs, or usage history. No third-party ad networks or tracking trackers are embedded.

6. App Version & Contact

This policy applies to AeroSense AI Version 1 (v1.0.0). For inquiries or questions, contact us via official project channels.
